WHO WE ARE

Rooted in Kolkata, Serving with Empathy Since 2015

Kolkata Hridam was established in Bijoygarh, Jadavpur by a group of socially conscious citizens determined to bridge health, educational, and economic disparities in West Bengal. From humble beginnings organizing neighbourhood eye-camps, Hridam has expanded into a reputable grassroots force.

WHAT WE DO

Our Key Areas of Grassroots Action

Through sustainable intervention models, we transform underprivileged lives across urban slums and rural hamlets in Bengal.

HEALTHCARE

Free Healthcare & Eye Camps

Organizing periodic free diagnostic checkups, diabetes & blood pressure screening, eye consultations, spectacle distribution, and blood donation drives with local partners.

120+ Camps Conducted
EDUCATION

Child Literacy & Study Centers

Providing free evening coaching, school bags, notebooks, textbooks, digital learning kits, and nutrition support to first-generation learners in underprivileged communities.

2,500+ Children Enrolled
WATER & SANITATION

Clean Drinking Water & Sanitation

Installing and repairing deep tube-wells, bio-sand water filters in salinity-prone and rural hamlets, along with community handwashing and hygiene drives.

30+ Clean Water Points
LIVELIHOOD

Women Livelihood & Vocational Skills

Conducting skill workshops in tailoring, embroidery, handicraft production, and small self-help group mentorship to empower rural women toward financial independence.

350+ Women Trained
RELIEF & RESCUE

Emergency Disaster & Cyclone Relief

Rapid deployment of food ration kits, drinking water pouches, tarpaulin roofing sheets, and medicine packets during natural calamities and river floods in Bengal.

15,000+ Ration Kits Delivered
ELDERLY CARE

Senior Care & Winter Warmth

Annual winter blanket drives, monthly medicinal support, nutrition packs, and companionship initiatives for destitute seniors who have no family support.

1,800+ Blankets & Aids
